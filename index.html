Support

Questions, problems with a subscription, or feedback? Email big14q@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

  • Cancel or manage Premium: in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or in the app under Me → Ember Premium.
  • New phone: your entries stay on the old phone. Export them first from Me → Your data. Premium comes back with "Restore purchases".
  • Forgot your PIN: use "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen.

Not medical care

Ember supports wellness and self-reflection. It is not medical or mental-health care. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
