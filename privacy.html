Ember

Privacy policy

Ember is built so your private life stays private. Everything you write and track stays on your phone.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up, and we never know who you are.
  • Your entries never leave your phone. Check-ins, cycle dates, journal, urges, streaks and program progress are stored only on your device, encrypted.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Ember contains no advertising or analytics tools.
  • The only thing that goes online is a subscription purchase, handled by Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat, tied to a random anonymous ID.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time in Me → Your data.

Who we are

Ember is made by Ember Wellness. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email big14q@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Ember is stored in a database on your device and nowhere else:

  • your daily check-ins (desire, mood, energy, notes and tags);
  • cycle information you choose to enter (last period date, cycle length);
  • journal entries, hard moments you log in the calm space, slips and streaks;
  • lesson, program and session progress, and your settings;
  • the name, phone number and message for "your person", if you add one.

The database is encrypted with a key that is created on your phone and kept in your phone's secure keystore. We cannot read it, and nor can anyone else without your unlocked phone. Ember turns off Android cloud backup for this data so it isn't copied to an online backup.

Because we never receive this information, we can't recover it if your phone is lost or reset. Use Me → Your data → Export to keep your own copy.

Features that run on your phone

  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The notification only says "Time for your check-in" and never mentions what Ember is about.
  • App lock (PIN, fingerprint or face) uses your phone's own security. Your PIN and biometric data are never sent to us. Biometric data never leaves your phone's secure hardware.
  • The voice guide plays recordings that are part of the app.
  • Texting or calling your person opens your phone's messages or phone app with the text filled in. Ember doesn't send anything itself, and the message is sent by you through your carrier.
  • Help links open the helpline's own website in your browser. Their privacy policies apply there.

Subscriptions (Ember Premium)

If you look at or buy Ember Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone). We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. For this, the following is processed:

  • a random, anonymous app ID created by RevenueCat (not linked to your name, email or phone number by us);
  • your purchase and subscription status, such as product, price, dates, trial and renewal;
  • basic technical information needed to make the request, such as device type, operating system version, app version, country or region and IP address.

We use this only to unlock Premium and to restore it on a new phone. None of your check-ins, journal or other entries are ever included. Google and Apple process payments under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details.

Store ratings

Occasionally Ember may show the app store's own rating window. If you leave a rating, it goes to Google Play or the App Store under their terms. Ember only stores, on your phone, when it last asked.

Why we process it (legal bases)

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply: subscription information is processed to provide the service you bought (contract). Everything else stays on your device under your control, so we don't process it.

How long it's kept

Your entries stay on your phone until you delete them or remove the app. Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat, Google and Apple for as long as needed to manage your subscription and to meet their legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Me → Your data → Export gives you a copy of everything.
  • Delete: Me → Your data → Delete removes all of it from your phone permanently. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Subscription data: to ask for access to or deletion of subscription records, email big14q@gmail.com. Because we don't know who you are, please include the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt so we can find the right record.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object, to restrict processing or to complain to your data-protection authority.

Age

Ember discusses desire and sexual wellbeing and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly provide it to anyone younger.

Not medical care

Ember supports wellness and self-reflection. It is not medical or mental-health care, and the cycle estimates are not for contraception or diagnosis. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a helpline. Ember lists some in Help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll also mention it in the app before it takes effect.
